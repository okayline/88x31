» The Ledger «

Every button in ~*~ 88x31 cLuB ~*~ has its own number, like a trading card. And EVERYTHING that happens to it gets written down in the Ledger: when it was found, every trade, every gift, every guestbook stamp. It's like a diary for your buttons!!

The cool part? Each new page of the diary is locked to the page before it with a secret fingerprint. Change even one tiny thing in the past and every fingerprint after it breaks. So nobody can sneak in and rewrite history... not even the webmaster!! ;)

What it means 4 you:

  • Your buttons are really yours. Anybody can save a picture, but only the Ledger knows whose copy is the real #12.
  • Shiny ones can't be faked. If the Ledger says it came out Holo, it came out Holo.
  • You can see where any button has been. Click one and check out its travel log!
  • Buttons are just for fun. They can never be bought or sold for real money. Ever.
